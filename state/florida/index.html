Florida Planting Calendar: Frost Dates, USDA Zone 9a–11a

Florida covers USDA zone 9a–11a. Average last spring frost Feb 10 to Feb 25. Average first fall frost Dec 5 to Dec 15.

Florida’s elevation runs from sea level at the Atlantic/Gulf coast up to 345 ft at Britton Hill, a 345 ft rise. Gardens near the top and bottom of that range can see real frost-date differences even inside the same USDA zone band, which is one reason to read the ZIP-level table above rather than a single state average.

Gap, stated plainly: zone 10a, zone 10b, zone 11a in Florida are outside our frost date set, so no dates are printed for them. We would rather show a blank than a guess.
