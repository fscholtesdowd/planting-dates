California’s elevation runs from 282 ft below sea level at Badwater Basin up to 14,505 ft at Mount Whitney — a 14,787 ft rise. Gardens near the top and bottom of that range can see real frost-date differences even inside the same USDA zone band, which is one reason to read the ZIP-level table above rather than a single state average.

Gap, stated plainly: zone 10a, zone 10b in California are outside our frost date set, so no dates are printed for them. We would rather show a blank than a guess.
