No single Hawaii calendar

Every zone we sampled in Hawaii sits outside our frost date set. Zones 10 and warmer rarely freeze at all, so a planting calendar built by counting weeks from a frost date does not apply there. Rather than invent one, we show nothing. For local sowing dates ask your county Extension office.

  • Want the real number for your town? Use NOAA's US Climate Normals (they include freeze and frost dates) or ask your county Extension office.
