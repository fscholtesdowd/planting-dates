Nevada’s elevation runs from 479 ft at the Colorado River up to 13,147 ft at Boundary Peak — a 12,668 ft rise. Gardens near the top and bottom of that range can see real frost-date differences even inside the same USDA zone band, which is one reason to read the ZIP-level table above rather than a single state average.

No single Nevada calendar

Nevada is split across too many zones to print one planting calendar honestly. Its most common zone, 9a, covers only 22% of the sample. Pick your own zone from the table above and open its calendar.
