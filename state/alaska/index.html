Alaska’s elevation runs from sea level at sea level on the Gulf of Alaska up to 20,310 ft at Denali, a 20,310 ft rise. Gardens near the top and bottom of that range can see real frost-date differences even inside the same USDA zone band, which is one reason to read the ZIP-level table above rather than a single state average.

Gap, stated plainly: zone 1b, zone 2a, zone 2b in Alaska are outside our frost date set, so no dates are printed for them. We would rather show a blank than a guess.

No single Alaska calendar

Alaska is split across too many zones to print one planting calendar honestly. Its most common zone, 5a, covers only 15% of the sample. Pick your own zone from the table above and open its calendar.
