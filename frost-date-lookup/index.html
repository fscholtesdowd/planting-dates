Find Your Frost Dates by ZIP Code

Type your ZIP code. This looks up your real USDA hardiness zone live from the government map, then shows that zone's average last spring frost and average first fall frost.

How this works

  1. Your ZIP code is sent to the USDA's own map, read through the free phzmapi.org mirror, the same source named on every page of this site.
  2. That returns your exact USDA hardiness zone.
  3. We show that zone's average last spring frost and average first fall frost, from the same dataset behind every zone page here.

These are zone averages, not a weather station reading for your exact street. They can run a week or two early or late. Your ZIP is sent straight to phzmapi.org and is not stored by this site.
