When to Plant Broccoli in Zone 6a

In USDA zone 6a, start broccoli indoors around Feb 24 and plant out around Mar 31. Worked out from zone 6a's average last spring frost, Apr 21.

Broccoli planting dates for zone 6a

Dataset row: Broccoli. Dates are zone averages counted from the Apr 21 last frost, not local records — frost varies a couple of weeks inside any zone.

WhatWhenHow it is worked out
Start seeds indoorsFeb 245 weeks before transplanting
Transplant outsideMar 31about 3 weeks before the last frost
Sow directly in the ground—normally transplanted
Fall sowing (sow by)Aug 111 weeks before the average first fall frost (Oct 17)

Broccoli likes cool weather and takes light frost. Heads bolt in heat, so the spring window matters more than for most crops.
