When to Plant Carrots in Zone 9b

In USDA zone 9b, sow carrots straight in the ground around Jan 20. Worked out from zone 9b's average last spring frost, Feb 10.

Carrots planting dates for zone 9b

Dataset row: Carrot. Dates are zone averages counted from the Feb 10 last frost, not local records — frost varies a couple of weeks inside any zone.

WhatWhenHow it is worked out
