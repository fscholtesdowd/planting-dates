When to Plant Eggplant in Zone 6b

In USDA zone 6b, start eggplant indoors around Feb 17 and plant out around Apr 28. Worked out from zone 6b's average last spring frost, Apr 14.

Eggplant planting dates for zone 6b

Dataset row: Eggplant. Dates are zone averages counted from the Apr 14 last frost, not local records — frost varies a couple of weeks inside any zone.

WhatWhenHow it is worked out
Start seeds indoorsFeb 1710 weeks before transplanting
Transplant outsideApr 28about 2 weeks after the last frost
Sow directly in the ground—normally transplanted

Eggplant wants the warmest spot you have. A cold snap can stall young plants for weeks, so later is safer than earlier.
