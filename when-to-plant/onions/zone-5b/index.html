When to Plant Onions in Zone 5b

In USDA zone 5b, start onions indoors around Feb 12 and plant out around Apr 9. Worked out from zone 5b's average last spring frost, May 7.

Onions planting dates for zone 5b

Dataset row: Onion (from seed). Dates are zone averages counted from the May 7 last frost, not local records — frost varies a couple of weeks inside any zone.

WhatWhenHow it is worked out
Start seeds indoorsFeb 128 weeks before transplanting
Transplant outsideApr 9about 4 weeks before the last frost
Sow directly in the ground—normally transplanted

These dates are for onions grown from seed, which need a long indoor head start. Sets (small bulbs) can go out around the same transplant date.
