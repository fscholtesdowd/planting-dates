When to Plant Beans in Zone 8b

In USDA zone 8b, sow beans straight in the ground around Mar 22. Worked out from zone 8b's average last spring frost, Mar 15.

Beans planting dates for zone 8b

Dataset row: Bean (bush and pole). Dates are zone averages counted from the Mar 15 last frost, not local records — frost varies a couple of weeks inside any zone.

WhatWhenHow it is worked out
Start seeds indoors—not usually started indoors
Transplant outside—normally direct sown
Sow directly in the groundMar 22about 1 week after the last frost
Fall sowing (sow by)Sep 139 weeks before the average first fall frost (Nov 15)

These dates cover bush and pole beans. The seeds rot in cold, wet soil, so there is no prize for sowing early.
