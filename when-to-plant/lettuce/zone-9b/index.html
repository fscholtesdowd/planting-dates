When to Plant Lettuce in Zone 9b

In USDA zone 9b, start lettuce indoors around Dec 30, plant out around Jan 27, or sow straight in the ground around Jan 27. Worked out from zone 9b's average last spring frost, Feb 10.

Dataset row: Lettuce. Dates are zone averages counted from the Feb 10 last frost, not local records — frost varies a couple of weeks inside any zone.
