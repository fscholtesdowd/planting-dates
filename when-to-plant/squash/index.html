When to Plant Squash in Every Zone (Summer and Winter Squash)

Squash hates frost. In every USDA zone the rule is the same: start seeds indoors 4 weeks before you plant out, plant out about 1 week after the last frost, or sow seeds straight in the ground about 2 weeks after the last frost. The dates change with your zone's last spring frost, so find your zone in the table below.

Summer squash or winter squash?

Same planting day, different harvest. Summer squash is picked young and soft, and the plant keeps making more all summer. Winter squash stays on the vine until the skin is hard, then it stores for months. If your season is short, summer squash is the safer bet. If you want food in January, grow winter squash and give it the whole season.

Which way should I plant it?

Squash does not like having its roots moved. Sowing straight in the ground is the easy way and the plants catch up fast. Start seeds indoors only if your season is short, use pots you can plant whole, and get them out on the transplant date before the roots wind up.
