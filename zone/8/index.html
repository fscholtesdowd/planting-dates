Zone 8 Planting Schedule — Frost Dates for 8a and 8b

USDA zone 8 splits into 8a and 8b. Average last spring frost Mar 25 in 8a and Mar 15 in 8b; average first fall frost Nov 8 and Nov 15.

Not sure which half you are in? Use the 8a dates — 8a is the colder half, its dates run later, and planting a week late costs nothing while a week early can cost the crop. Or find your exact half zone from your state page.
