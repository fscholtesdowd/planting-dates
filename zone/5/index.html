Zone 5 Planting Schedule — Frost Dates for 5a and 5b

USDA zone 5 splits into 5a and 5b. Average last spring frost May 15 in 5a and May 7 in 5b; average first fall frost Oct 1 and Oct 12.

Not sure which half you are in? Use the 5a dates — 5a is the colder half, its dates run later, and planting a week late costs nothing while a week early can cost the crop. Or find your exact half zone from your state page.
