Zone 9 Planting Schedule: Frost Dates for 9a and 9b

USDA zone 9 splits into 9a and 9b. Average last spring frost Feb 25 in 9a and Feb 10 in 9b; average first fall frost Dec 5 and Dec 15.

Not sure which half you are in? Use the 9a dates: 9a is the colder half, its dates run later, and planting a week late costs nothing while a week early can cost the crop. Or find your exact half zone from your state page.
